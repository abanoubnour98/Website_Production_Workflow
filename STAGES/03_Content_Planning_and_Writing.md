# Stage 03 — Content Planning & Writing

**بالعربية:** تخطيط وكتابة المحتوى  
**رقم المرحلة:** 03 من 13

## 1. الهدف

تنفيذ مرحلة **Content Planning & Writing** ضمن منهجية بناء الموقع، وتسليم مخرجات قابلة للمراجعة والاستخدام في المرحلة التالية.

## 2. المهارات المطلوبة (Skills)

Content Strategy, Content Briefs, SEO Copywriting, Content Writing, Semantic SEO, Editorial Writing, Fact-Checking, Localization, Arabic/English Editing

## 3. يعتمد على (Previous Inputs)

Stages 01–02: approved business brief, audience needs, competitor research, sitemap, keyword-to-URL map, search intent and internal-linking blueprint.

> قبل التشغيل، أرفق الملفات الفعلية للمراحل السابقة. لا تعتمد على افتراض أن الـAgent يتذكر محادثة أو يستطيع فتح ملفات غير مرفقة.

## 4. المهام المطلوبة

1. Create a content inventory and prioritize every approved page.
2. Prepare a page-specific content brief with audience, intent, primary topic, supporting topics, entities, headings and required elements.
3. Research competing ranking pages to understand useful depth, format, missing information and user expectations.
4. Write original copy that answers the main user need early and develops the topic logically.
5. Create unique title tags, meta descriptions, H1/H2/H3 structure, introductions, body sections, tables, FAQs and relevant CTAs where suitable.
6. Localize each language naturally; adapt examples, terminology, tone and reading conventions rather than translating literally.
7. Add contextual internal-link suggestions, authoritative citations for factual claims and notes for facts requiring verification.
8. Run editorial review for accuracy, clarity, originality, helpfulness, consistency and compliance.

## 5. المخرجات المطلوبة (Deliverables)

- Content inventory and editorial priorities
- Page-by-page content briefs
- Complete page copy in requested languages
- SEO titles and meta descriptions
- Heading structures, FAQs and CTA copy
- Internal-link anchors and target recommendations
- Sources and fact-verification notes
- Editorial QA report and content status tracker

## 6. معايير القبول (Acceptance Criteria)

- [ ] Every approved page has a defined audience, intent and content purpose.
- [ ] Content is original, useful, accurate and not padded to meet arbitrary word counts.
- [ ] Metadata is unique and accurately describes each page.
- [ ] Arabic and other localized content reads naturally and has been edited.
- [ ] Claims requiring evidence have citations or are flagged for verification.
- [ ] The content is ready for UX layout and design review.

## 7. Tips & Best Practices

- Do not set universal word counts; determine depth from search intent, topic complexity and user value.
- Put the direct answer or page value near the beginning where appropriate.
- Use headings that help scanning, not headings inserted only to repeat keywords.
- Never invent reviews, testimonials, author credentials, legal permissions, licensing or product features.
- Avoid machine-translated Arabic without native editorial review.
- Keep content and page structure flexible enough for UX to improve presentation without changing the search intent.

For each approved page, create a clear content brief covering its target audience, primary search intent, main topic, related topics, entities, unique value, required sections, internal links, authoritative sources and CTA requirements. Review relevant competitor pages and current SERP formats to understand the information users expect. Learn from their structure and useful page elements, but write original content and never copy their wording.

Write complete, accurate, helpful page content in every requested language. Include an appropriate SEO title, meta description, URL recommendation where needed, H1, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, introduction, main sections, tables or lists where they improve comprehension, FAQs where genuinely useful, relevant CTA copy and contextual internal-link suggestions. Localize naturally for each market; do not translate literally. Cite material factual claims and flag legal, financial, medical, product or other sensitive claims for verification by an appropriate source or expert. Do not invent credentials, reviews, customer experiences, licenses, availability, statistics or guarantees. Do not use arbitrary word-count targets or keyword stuffing.

Deliver page-by-page files and a content inventory showing status, language, target URL, intent, keyword mapping, sources and review status. Content must be ready for wireframing and UX review.

PHASE OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS
Create every deliverable listed in the stage file. Use clear filenames and include the project name, stage number and version/date where appropriate. If file creation is unavailable, provide complete structured content that can be saved as files.

QUALITY GATE
Evaluate every acceptance criterion in the stage file. Mark PASS, FAIL or NOT TESTED. Do not claim completion for unverified work. Fix what you can and document the remaining blockers.

HANDOFF
