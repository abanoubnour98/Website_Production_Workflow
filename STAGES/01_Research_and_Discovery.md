# Stage 01 — Research & Discovery

**بالعربية:** البحث والاستكشاف  
**رقم المرحلة:** 01 من 13

## 1. الهدف

تنفيذ مرحلة **Research & Discovery** ضمن منهجية بناء الموقع، وتسليم مخرجات قابلة للمراجعة والاستخدام في المرحلة التالية.

## 2. المهارات المطلوبة (Skills)

Market Research, Competitor Analysis, Audience Research, Business Analysis, UX Research, Industry Research

## 3. يعتمد على (Previous Inputs)

Project Configuration (00_PROJECT_CONFIGURATION.md). Previous website data, analytics, interviews, documents and existing assets when available.

> قبل التشغيل، أرفق الملفات الفعلية للمراحل السابقة. لا تعتمد على افتراض أن الـAgent يتذكر محادثة أو يستطيع فتح ملفات غير مرفقة.

## 4. المهام المطلوبة

1. Understand the business model, offerings, value proposition, objectives and project constraints.
2. Research the industry, market segments, audience needs, problems, expectations and decision factors.
3. Identify direct, indirect and aspirational competitors; inspect their actual websites and relevant pages.
4. Compare competitor positioning, navigation, content types, user journeys, trust signals and visible UX patterns.
5. Review available first-party evidence such as analytics, Search Console, customer feedback, sales data and existing site content.
6. Document opportunities, risks, assumptions, unknowns and questions requiring stakeholder input.

## 5. المخرجات المطلوبة (Deliverables)

- Project brief and scope
- Market and audience research report
- Audience personas or audience segments
- Competitor matrix with source URLs and observations
- Business requirements and success goals
- Assumptions, risks and open-questions register

## 6. معايير القبول (Acceptance Criteria)

- [ ] Business purpose, audience and primary goals are documented.
- [ ] Competitors are real and findings have source links or evidence.
- [ ] Facts are separated from assumptions and recommendations.
- [ ] Scope, constraints, risks and unresolved questions are visible.

## 7. Tips & Best Practices

- Prefer first-party data and primary sources over generic industry claims.
- Study competitor patterns, not just their appearance; record what the pattern helps users achieve.
- Do not infer competitor traffic, conversions or rankings without reliable data.
- Keep a short list of the most relevant competitors rather than collecting many unrelated websites.

## 8. READY-TO-USE PROMPT

انسخ الـPrompt التالي، وألحق به `00_MASTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md` و`00_PROJECT_CONFIGURATION.md` ومخرجات المراحل السابقة المطلوبة أعلاه.

```text
PHASE OUTPUT REQUIREMENTS

QUALITY GATE
Evaluate every acceptance criterion in the stage file. Mark PASS, FAIL or NOT TESTED. Do not claim completion for unverified work. Fix what you can and document the remaining blockers.

HANDOFF
Prepare a handoff for the next stage. List exact deliverable filenames, key decisions, assumptions, unresolved issues, dependencies and any approvals needed. Do not execute the next stage.
```
